White Spot Disease ⚪

🔍 Scientific Cause

  • Pathogen: Often Fungal (e.g., Oidium spp.)
  • Thrives in high humidity with low light
  • Poor air circulation around the base
  • Spread by spores through wind or water

⚠️ Key Symptoms

  • Circular white or gray powdery spots
  • Spots enlarge and cover the whole leaf
  • Leaves eventually turn yellow and brittle
  • Stunted growth of new chili pods

🚶 Immediate Actions

  • Prune and remove heavily infected leaves
  • Stop overhead irrigation (keep leaves dry)
  • Improve sunlight exposure to the plant
  • Clear weeds from the base of the plant

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • Ensure wide spacing between plants
  • Use clean, sterilized gardening tools
  • Plant in areas with good morning sunlight
  • Apply organic mulch to prevent spore splash

💊 Recommended Fungicides

Chemical Type Dosage Application
Carbendazim Fungicide 1 g/L Spray thoroughly on both sides of leaves
Wettable Sulfur Fungicide 2 g/L Effective for early stages
Mancozeb Fungicide 2.5 g/L Spray every 10 days until clear

* White spots spread very fast in the shade. Move plants to the sun if possible!
