Chili Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuv) 🌪️

🔍 Scientific Cause

  • Vector: Transmitted by Whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci)
  • Infection spreads as bugs feed on different plants
  • Virus lives inside the plant's vascular system
  • Common in warm, dry weather conditions

⚠️ Key Symptoms

  • Upward curling of leaf margins
  • Leaves become puckered, small, and twisted
  • Plants appear bushy and severely stunted
  • Flower buds drop before they can turn into chilis

🚶 Immediate Actions

  • Uproot and burn infected plants immediately
  • Do not keep infected plants in a compost pile
  • Set up yellow sticky traps to catch whiteflies
  • Check neighboring plants for early curling

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • Plant virus-resistant chili varieties
  • Use fine nylon nets in nurseries
  • Remove weeds like 'Datura' which host the virus
  • Avoid planting chilis near tobacco or tomato crops

💊 Insect Control (To Stop Spread)

Treatment Type Dosage Application
Imidacloprid Systemic Insecticide 0.5 ml/L Spray early morning to kill whiteflies
Neem Oil Spray Organic Repellent 3–5 ml/L Natural way to keep bugs away
Thiamethoxam Insecticide 0.25 g/L Effective for severe infestations

* Note: Viruses cannot be cured. You must focus on killing the insects that carry them!
