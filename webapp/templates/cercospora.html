Cercospora Leaf Spot (Frogeye) 🐸

🔍 Scientific Cause

  • Fungus: Cercospora capsici
  • Spread by wind and rain splash
  • Lives in infected plant debris
  • Thrives in high humidity and warm climates

⚠️ Key Symptoms

  • Small circular spots (Frogeye look)
  • Light gray or brown centers
  • Dark reddish-brown borders around spots
  • Heavy yellowing leads to leaves falling off

🚶 Immediate Actions

  • Remove and burn infected leaves immediately
  • Avoid watering the plants from above (Foliage)
  • Improve air circulation by pruning
  • Clean up fallen leaves from the soil

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • Use high-quality, disease-free seeds
  • Ensure proper spacing between plants
  • Practice 2-3 year crop rotation
  • Remove all plant debris after harvest

💊 Recommended Fungicides & Dosage

Chemical Type Dosage Application
Mancozeb Fungicide 2–2.5 g/L Spray every 7–10 days
Chlorothalonil Fungicide 2 g/L Full foliar spray coverage
Copper Oxychloride Fungicide 2–3 g/L Use as a preventive spray

* Proper field sanitation is the best way to control fungal spread.
