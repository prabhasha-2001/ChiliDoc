Bacterial Spot 🦠

🔍 Scientific Cause

  • Bacteria: Xanthomonas campestris
  • Spreads through infected seeds and water splash
  • Enters plants through wounds or natural openings
  • Favors warm, wet, and windy weather

⚠️ Key Symptoms

  • Small, water-soaked spots on leaves
  • Spots turn dark brown with yellow halos
  • Scabby, raised spots on the chili fruit
  • Severe cases cause leaves to turn yellow and drop

🚶 Immediate Actions

  • Remove and destroy infected plants immediately
  • Disinfect garden tools with alcohol or bleach
  • Stop overhead watering to prevent splashing
  • Avoid handling plants when they are wet

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • Buy certified disease-free seeds
  • Practice a 3-year crop rotation (no peppers/tomatoes)
  • Space plants widely for better air flow
  • Mulch the soil to prevent bacteria splash-back

💊 Recommended Bactericides

Treatment Type Dosage Application
Copper Oxychloride Bactericide 2–3 g/L Spray every 7–10 days in wet weather
Copper + Mancozeb Mix Treatment Standard Mix Combining helps manage resistance
Streptomycin Sulfate Antibiotic 0.5 g/L Use only for severe nursery outbreaks

* Bacterial spot can survive in the soil for years. Always rotate your crops!
