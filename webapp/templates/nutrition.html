Nutrition Deficiency 🧪

🔍 Why does this happen?

  • Lack of Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), or Potassium (K)
  • Soil pH level is too high or too low
  • Leaching of nutrients due to heavy rain
  • Poor soil quality or lack of organic compost

⚠️ Key Signs

  • Yellowing leaves: (Lack of Nitrogen)
  • Purple leaf edges: (Lack of Phosphorus)
  • Burnt leaf tips: (Lack of Potassium)
  • Slow growth and small, weak chili pods

🚶 Quick Fixes

  • Test your soil pH to check acidity.
  • Add high-quality compost or cow manure.
  • Apply liquid fertilizer for faster absorption.
  • Loosen the soil around the roots for better air.

🛡️ Long-term Prevention

  • Apply balanced NPK fertilizer every 2 weeks.
  • Mix organic matter into the soil before planting.
  • Maintain a consistent watering schedule.
  • Rotate crops to keep the soil healthy.

📋 Recommended Nutrients & Dosage

Nutrient Best Source Dosage Application
Nitrogen (N) Urea / Compost 2–3 g/L Apply to soil or foliar spray
Phosphorus (P) Superphosphate 2 g/L Mix into soil near root zone
Potassium (K) Muriate of Potash 2 g/L Apply during fruiting stage
Magnesium (Mg) Epsom Salt 1 g/L Spray on leaves once a month

* Tip: Organic compost is the best long-term solution for nutrient-rich soil!
