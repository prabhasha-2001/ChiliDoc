🚀 How to Maximize Your Harvest

💎 Recommended Fertilizers

  • K44 K44 (High Potassium): Apply every 2 weeks after flowering begins. It increases chili size, weight, and color vibrancy.
  • Albert's Albert's Solution: A complete micronutrient mix. Best applied as a foliar spray to ensure the plant stays lush and green.
  • TDM Top Dressing Mixture: Use during the early growth phase to promote bushier plants and more branches.

🌸 Flower & Pod Protection

Prevent flower drop and encourage more fruit by using Boron-enriched liquid fertilizers. Ensure consistent watering during the fruiting stage; dry soil can cause young chilies to fall off.

⭐ Pro Tip: Boosting potassium levels during peak bloom can increase your total yield weight by up to 30%!

Healthy Pods

📝 Daily Maintenance

  • ✔ Maintain moist soil (water at the base, not the leaves).
  • ✔ Check daily for pests like aphids or thrips under leaves.
  • ✔ Prune any yellowing bottom leaves to improve air flow.

☀️ Sunlight & Air

Chili plants love the sun. Ensure at least 6-8 hours of direct sunlight for the best spice and flavor.

Keep spacing between plants wide to allow air to dry any dew, preventing fungal growth.
